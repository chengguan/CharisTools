The idea

Most free PDF editors edit more than you asked them to.

Point at existing text to fix a typo and the whole line reflows. Sign a form and a watermark shows up anyway. CharisPDF adds your marks on top of the page and leaves everything else exactly as it was.

Add, don't rewrite

Text, shapes, and signatures land on the page as objects you can still select later — move, resize, restyle, or delete them. The original content stream is never touched.

Private by default

No account, no upload, no analytics. We never send your PDF. Share, AirDrop, or your own iCloud only happen when you do.

Built for an agent, too

App Intents and Shortcuts let your own agent search snippets, count pages, rotate, add text, or undo on the open PDF. CharisPDF supplies the tools — you keep the file.

User guide

Five steps. No account.

CharisPDF only adds marks on top of your PDF. It does not rewrite the original page content, and it does not require a sign-in.

1

Open a PDF

Launch CharisPDF → tap a recent file, use Browse, or open a PDF from Files / Mail / another app with Share → CharisPDF. Or tap New PDF for a blank page.

2

Sign

In the editor toolbar, choose the signature tool. Draw or type a signature as an overlay you can move or delete later. A photo signature flattens onto that page (PDFKit limit) and is not selectable afterward.

3

Add text or markup

Add text boxes and shapes as overlays. Highlight, underline, or strike through existing text without changing the underlying page stream.

4

Organize pages

Reorder, rotate, insert, delete, extract, or duplicate pages when you need a cleaner packet — still on device.

5

Save

Save back to Files (or Save a Copy). Undo/redo through this session. Works offline; dark mode supported.

Privacy: CharisPDF collects no analytics and does not upload your files. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. Optional Shortcuts / App Intents are on device and do not upload the PDF.

Questions

Before you install it.

How do I sign a PDF on iPhone without an account?

Open the PDF in CharisPDF, pick the signature tool, then draw or type your signature — no sign-in of any kind. It lands as an overlay you can select, move, or delete later. A photo signature is the one exception: PDFKit flattens it onto the page, so it isn't selectable again after you save.

How do I highlight or underline text in a PDF for free?

Pick the highlight, underline, or strikethrough tool and mark the existing text directly. It only marks the text — the original page content underneath is never rewritten or reflowed.

Does editing a PDF in CharisPDF change the original text?

No — that's the whole design rule. CharisPDF only ever adds new objects (text, shapes, signatures, highlights) on top of the page. It won't touch, reflow, or replace the original content stream, even to fix a typo.

Does CharisPDF save my changes automatically?

Not yet. CharisPDF doesn't autosave — tap Save (or Save a Copy) before you switch apps or put your phone away. iOS can close a backgrounded app without warning, and unsaved edits would be lost. Autosave is a known gap, not a hidden one.

Is CharisPDF really free, with no in-app purchases?

Yes. No subscription, no watermark on what you save, and no in-app purchases in this version.

Does CharisPDF upload my PDF anywhere?

No. Everything happens on-device — no analytics, no upload. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. The one exception is if you deliberately connect your own AI agent via Shortcuts/App Intents, which is optional and off by default.
