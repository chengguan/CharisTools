## Apps

### [CharisPDF](CharisPDF/)

Free, local, account-free PDF editor for iPhone and iPad. Add text, shapes, and signatures to any PDF — original page content is never rewritten, and your files never leave your device. [Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/charispdf/id6803359246).

### [CharisRemote](CharisRemote/)

Remote control for Android TV / Google TV on iPhone and iPad. No cloud, no account, no ads — pairs directly with your TV over your own Wi-Fi. Free core remote forever; a one-time purchase unlocks touchpad and shortcuts.
