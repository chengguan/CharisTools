The idea

Most TV remote apps route your button presses through someone's cloud.

CharisRemote doesn't. It speaks your TV's own remote-control protocol directly, over the Wi-Fi network you're already on — the same way Google's own remote app does, minus the account.

Pairing stays on your Wi-Fi

There's no server this app talks to for pairing or control. Key presses, volume, and typing travel straight from your phone to your TV over an encrypted local connection. The one exception: the optional Pro purchase, which Apple's own StoreKit handles directly.

Pair once, reconnect instantly

Your device's pairing identity lives in the iPhone's Keychain. Come back tomorrow, or after a phone restart, and CharisRemote just reconnects — no PIN, no re-pairing.

Wi-Fi only, by design

CharisRemote refuses to connect over cellular data, and refuses any address that isn't on your local network — even if you type one in by hand.
